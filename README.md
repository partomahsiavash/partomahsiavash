## About

I am an AI research engineer and scientific programmer working at the intersection of **machine learning**, **deep learning**, and **computational engineering**. My work spans computer vision, data science, cybersecurity, operator learning for partial differential equations, medical imaging, and AI-assisted experimental analysis.

I currently contribute to academic and applied research at **Sahand University of Technology** and the **Sahand Oil and Gas Research Institute**. Petroleum and reservoir engineering are important application domains within my broader AI and scientific-computing work.
